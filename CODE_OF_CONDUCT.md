# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Examples include:

- Respect different viewpoints.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on ideas and technical issues rather than individuals.
- Accept correction professionally.
- Help maintain a collaborative environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing,
threats, and other harmful conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action to address violations,
including warnings, moderation, or removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report serious conduct issues through the project's designated community
contact channel.
